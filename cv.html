Employment

  • 2025 – now
    Postdoctoral researcher

    Chromatin regulation and neural development in Nematostella vectensis. Gahan Lab (PI: James Gahan), Centre for Chromosome Biology, University of Galway, Ireland.

  • Nov 2024 – 2025
    Research associate

    Feuda Lab, Department of Genetics, Genomics and Cancer Sciences, University of Leicester, UK. Applied single-cell multiomics to the neuronal specification programme of the Drosophila embryo, validated with HCR and immunofluorescence, and built a new cell-type atlas of the sponge Sycon ciliatum.

Education

  • 2020 – 2024
    PhD in Genetics

    How to build a nervous system? Understanding the genetic control of neurogenesis in Drosophila melanogaster: insights using comparative and systematic approaches. Supervisor: Roberto Feuda. University of Leicester, UK. Funded by a BBSRC MIBTP studentship. Built a time-course, FACS-enriched single-cell atlas of embryonic neurogenesis and compared neurogenic gene regulatory networks across bilaterians.

  • 2016 – 2020
    BSc in Genetics, First Class Honours

    University College Dublin, Ireland. Final-year thesis in the O’Sullivan Lab modelling hereditary spastic paraplegia in Drosophila and human cells; research module in the Butler Lab sequencing yeasts from Irish soils.

Skills

Wet lab

Single-cell RNA-seq (10x Genomics), hybridisation chain reaction (HCR), in situ hybridisation, immunofluorescence, confocal microscopy, CRISPR gene editing, cloning, PCR and Western blotting, FACS, Drosophila husbandry and genetics, Nematostella culture, basic human cell culture.

Bioinformatics

Single-cell and bulk RNA-seq analysis, genome and transcriptome assembly, genome annotation, phylogenetics and phylogenomics. R, Python and bash; Git; Linux and high-performance computing; ImageJ.
